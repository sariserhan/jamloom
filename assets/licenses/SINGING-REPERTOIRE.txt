Jamloom Singing Academy repertoire

Original songs, English practice lyrics for Ode to Joy, and new accompaniment:
prepared for Jamloom. No outside vocal recordings are included.

Ode to Joy: L. van Beethoven (1770–1827).
Melody source: Mutopia-2009/08/05-528, typeset by Peter Chubb.
https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=528
The source score is dedicated to the public domain by its contributor.
https://www.mutopiaproject.org/legal.html
Jamloom transposes the melody, lifts the bridge cadence one octave, supplies
new English practice lyrics and renders a new accompaniment.

Frère Jacques: traditional French melody and lyrics.
Melody checked against Noctave's CC0 1.0 edition:
https://noctavepiano.com/en/sheet-music/trad-frere-jacques/
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Jamloom transposes the melody, raises the closing bell one octave and adds
its own backing. Two complete passes are used for practice.
No Noctave audio, branding, artwork or application code is included.
