# Jamloom launch kit

Drafts for the owner to post. Every claim matches the live product on 2026-11-16; re-check prices and the free-song offer before posting.

## One-line positioning

Pick artists you love, press one button, get an original AI song with vocals — in your language. No prompt writing.

## Facts to use (and only these)

- Music DNA: favorite artists or songs (or a YouTube playlist) → original song, about 2 minutes, AI vocals, 50 singing languages.
- Improvise: record a hum, beat or sentence → 30–45 second arrangement around it.
- One click: press Generate, browse or close the page, get an email when it is ready (usually 5–15 minutes in total).
- Price: 20 credits per public song, 25 private. $20 for 150 credits, or Creator $35/month for 300. Credits never expire; failed songs refund.
- Free first song for new accounts while places last (66 places).
- Not a copy: inspired by favorites' style, never their recordings, melodies or voices.

Do not claim: instant results, unlimited songs, commercial rights, "sounds exactly like" any artist, or numbers of users.
