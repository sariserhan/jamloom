# Request for the data-sourcing agent

Jamloom is building a DJ that generates original club music and performs it with
real deck operations. Research two separate datasets, rather than assuming one
finished-song corpus teaches both composition and DJ control.

1. **Original music generation:** human-made driving house, tech house and techno;
   primarily instrumental, with occasional vocal hooks. Prefer full tracks plus
   aligned drums/kick/bass/music/vocal stems, tempo, downbeats, phrases, builds,
   drops, breakdowns, energy and key annotations. Do not imitate a named artist.
2. **DJ decisions:** expert performances paired with the exact source tracks/stems,
   resulting mix, timestamped deck/MIDI actions and deck state, candidate track
   choices, vocal/phrase context and quality annotations. Include deliberately
   doing nothing, recovery, energy requests and party endings. Finished mixes
   without action recordings are weaker evidence; identify that limitation.

For each candidate give the original publisher URL, exact license and evidence
covering commercial training/fine-tuning, composition and master rights, performer
consent, third-party samples, model retention and commercial generated outputs.
Separate licenses for code, annotations, model weights and audio. Do not assume
streaming access, ordinary purchases or a royalty-free label grants training rights.
Report dataset size, download size, cost, annotation coverage, provenance, quality
checks, independent performer/session/track-family splits and missing permissions.

Only free, commercially permissible data is in scope. Start with ETH DISCO
EDM-CUE, Groove MIDI, original CC0 material and voluntary cleared demonstrations.
Exclude GCX and Rights Foundry offerings that require paid commercial licenses. EDM-CUE metadata is already staged locally; it contains no audio or full
action traces. UnmixDB/Mixotic noncommercial terms and DJ Mix Dataset audio rights
remain unresolved for our commercial use. Do not mistake a paper's CC license for
its underlying audio license.

Exclude Blood Club, excerpts, adapters and derivatives. It was only an example
of desired energy and production quality. No sampling or training use is authorized
for this effort.

Deliver a ranked shortlist, a proposed small pilot, an evidence-linked rights
matrix, exact unresolved questions, and draft inquiries to the relevant providers
or recording partners. Include failure/no-op examples and held-out expert review
in the pilot. Do not claim a small pilot covers every DJ technique. Separate
published prices from estimates and quotes still required.

Do not purchase, contact anyone, accept contracts, scrape restricted audio or
download large archives. The Mac has 16 GB unified memory and limited disk space.
Vast.ai compute is allowed within the existing authorized budget; this does not
authorize paid data or new infrastructure. Research public metadata first. Do not edit Jamloom's code; the implementation
agent is working on it independently. Return source evidence and proposed next
steps for review.
