# Revenue-funded GPU capacity

Owner decision, November 9, 2026: reserve 20% of net payment proceeds for GPU usage; keep one L4 worker initially.

The verified Stripe purchase adapter records gross amount, actual processor fee and tax separately from the wallet’s conservative planning reserves. The GPU allocation is `(gross cents - fee cents - tax cents) * 10000 / 5`, floored at zero. USD micros avoid floating-point accumulation. Receipt, charge and balance-transaction IDs are unique. Financial evidence survives account anonymization.

Pending payments create pending allocations. The five-minute operations monitor rereads Stripe’s captured live charge and balance transaction; only available funds become spendable. Test, trial, promotional and estimated Apple receipts cannot fund this pool. Apple financial reports are read daily; positive proceeds remain unspendable until a reviewed bank-payment confirmation matches the report. Negative corrections and verified live refunds reduce or hold Apple funding. No Apple proceeds are currently available. Refunds/disputes revoke the whole original allocation conservatively, including partial refunds. Already reserved costs remain recorded; negative available funds must be covered by later revenue before further revenue-funded starts.

The original $10/seven-day seed grant retains its exact dates and thirteen-cycle limit. While valid it funds cycles first. `JAMLOOM_GPU_REVENUE_SHARE_BPS=2000` allows subsequent cycles from the separate durable revenue pool. The seed grant’s stored hash stays unchanged, while the guardian policy hash changes. Disabling the flag restores finite-only admission and stops cycles after the old expiry. Never delete the revenue ledger or reuse spent funds during a rollout or rollback.

Each explicit preparation reserves its entire $0.75 maximum before activating the one L4 worker. Concurrent starts share the recorded cycle. Lost activation responses retain their reservation and trigger stop-only recovery. Shutdown never invents a refund from an estimated runtime. Existing startup, idle, lease, per-owner and job-credit cost limits stay enforced. More revenue buys additional funded preparation cycles, not additional simultaneous workers.

Runpod prepaid credit is checked before each activation; a fresh monitor read also gates new sales. The owner separately approved native card auto-pay: $25 below a $10 balance, with low-balance notifications below $5. This recurring prepaid cash buffer does not transfer Stripe money directly, create revenue allocations or expand GPU usage authority. The operating monitor verifies the exact native policy and alerts on drift. Funding exhaustion closes new sales and preparation; existing paid credits, result access, cancellation and receipt recovery remain available. No first-payment bootstrap is possible after all funding is exhausted without an authorized seed or newly settled earlier payments.

The 65% contribution target is still unqualified. Creator collections remain paused after the initial payment, as disclosed, with no catch-up billing. Guarded renewal continuation is configured but cannot resume until actual economic qualification and fresh funded capacity pass; it removes only Jamloom’s own pause on supported current-catalog subscriptions. Actual Apple proceeds require payment reconciliation. A 20% allocation is a spending rule, not evidence of margin or complete credit-liability coverage.
