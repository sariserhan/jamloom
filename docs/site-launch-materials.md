# Jamloom website launch materials

## Main message

**Make a song. Make a mix. Make it yours.**

Start with a sound, your favorite artists or two tracks. Find your own way to make music with Jamloom.

Primary destination: https://www.jamloom.com/start
Listening destination: https://www.jamloom.com/community
Interactive product tour: https://www.jamloom.com/tour

## Community announcement

**Your next repeat was made here.**

Explore original songs and full DJ sets on Jamloom. Find a style you like, save a favorite and keep listening while you explore. Your next creation could be here too.

## Product captions

- **Improvise:** Record a sound. Choose a style. Hear it become a song.
- **Music DNA:** Start with the songs and artists you love. Make something original.
- **Local Remixes:** Your sound, a new groove. Free, on your device.
- **DJ Studio:** Two tracks. Your first mix. No controller needed.
- **Live DJ:** Bring permitted live Mac audio to your decks.
- **DJ Academy:** Follow a lesson. Blend two tracks. Hear your progress.

Creation previews, paid cloud generation and course sales retain their release gates. Use the current availability shown in the product tour when publishing an announcement. Do not describe AI DJ as a fully qualified nonstop venue service yet.

## Real demonstration

Open `/tour`, scroll to **Try a real crossfade**, press Play on both decks and move Mix from A to B. The running product supplies the audio and control behavior. Playback is stopped by default.

For a shareable generated song: open Community, choose a song page, then use **Share song**, **Cover artwork**, or **Make 15-second video**. No model render or paid call is needed for these exports.
