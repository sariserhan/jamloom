    Listen, sing, repeat

    For a song, listen to the piano melody guide with the band, then choose Sing along and follow the lyrics. For a warm-up, hear the example, then hum or sing “ah” when it is your turn. Choose a register that feels comfortable.

    The marker moves left when you are low and right when you are high. For a hold, keep it centered for the shown time. For a melody, enter with each highlighted note and hold its pitch. Stay quiet during rests. Every note and rest must qualify; the result explains what to practice. If no clear note is detected, move closer to the microphone or try a quieter room. On a Mac, compare Standard Mic Mode with Voice Isolation if sustained notes disappear; the browser cannot inspect that system setting.

    The check measures pitch, not vocal health or overall singing quality. Another pitched sound can also match; practice with your voice. Song practice plays the original band arrangement while you sing. Follow the lyrics and the live pitch trace. Hear song adds a piano melody guide; these practice arrangements do not include a recorded singer. You can repeat as often as you like after passing.

    Stop, leaving the page or switching tabs turns the microphone off. Only lesson scores and completion are saved on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Practice never runs out. “Hear it first” plays the example without opening the microphone.
