Your first steady note

Choose a note that feels comfortable. Jamloom plays it on its piano, then goes quiet while you hum or sing “ah”.

The marker moves left when you are low and right when you are high. Keep it in the center for two seconds to complete the lesson. If no clear note is detected, move closer to the microphone or try a quieter room.

The check measures pitch, not vocal health or overall singing quality. Another pitched sound can also match; practice with your voice. You can repeat as often as you like after passing.

Stop, leaving the page or switching tabs turns the microphone off. Only a completion check is saved on this device.
